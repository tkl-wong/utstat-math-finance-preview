Members of the University of Toronto Mathematical Finance group are organizing *The Future of Mathematical Finance: Celebrating U of T at 200* (FMF200), taking place May 26–28, 2027 at the University of Toronto.

The three-day conference will celebrate the University's bicentennial by bringing together the mathematical finance community for plenary and invited talks, research exchange, and discussion about the future of the field.

[Visit the FMF200 website for more information](https://fmf200.utstat.utoronto.ca/index.html).
